Cart & Checkout Overview

Learn how to use Cart, Checkout, and Payment to enable the purchase of Nike products and services in your app.

Consumers purchase quickly and easily using Nike Cart, Checkout, and Payment

Nike builds digital experiences that showcase its premium products and tell amazing stories. Powering those experiences are Nike Cart, Checkout, and Payment, built to handle the massive scale of global e-commerce.

Adding Cart, Checkout, and Payment To Your App or Experience

The consumer finds a Nike product that they love in your app. What happens next? Step through the check list below to incorporate Cart, Checkout, and Payment capabilities into your experience.

1  Manage a consumer’s shopping cart and get product pricing
2  Manage a consumer’s wishlist (member/employee only) of products and services
3  Review a consumer’s cart for checkout
4  Get available shipping options or get available fulfillment offerings
          See Which API Version Should I Use for more details.
5  Validate a shipping address
6  Validate a checkout for fulfillment
7  Get available payment methods
8  Validate payment
9  Submit a checkout for fulfillment

Which API Version Should I Use?

The Nike shopping experience involves several APIs. Your app or experience implements one of two fulfillment flows, legacy or omni-channel.

The legacy flow allows consumers to choose how quickly they want all products delivered to a shipping address.

The omni-channel flow expands the fulfillment options to include ship, digital delivery, and pick up for each item based on consumer location, giving consumers much more flexibility in how, when, and where they can receive Nike products.

The APIs required for the two fulfillment flows are listed below.
