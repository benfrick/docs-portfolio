Cart and Checkout Overview

Learn how to use Cart and Checkout, our solution to allow customers to purchase Nike products and services.

  • Developer's Guide

Nike Checkout gives customers fast and easy access to make purchases

Nike builds digital experiences that showcase its premium products and tell amazing stories. Powering those experiences is Nike Checkout, built to handle the massive scale of global e-commerce.

Drive traffic and engagement in your app by leveraging Nike Checkout today.


Use Cases

Your customer finds a Nike a product they love in your app. What happens next? Step through the Use Cases below to incorporate Cart and Checkout capabilities into your experience.

  Manage a customer’s shopping cart and get product pricing
  Manage a customer’s Wish Lists (member/employee only) of products and services they may buy in the future
  Check the ‘buyability’ of a product
  Review a customer’s cart for checkout
  Get available shipping methods and estimated delivery dates
  Validate a checkout for fulfillment
  Submit a checkout for fulfillment
