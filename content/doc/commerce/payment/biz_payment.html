Overview

The Nike Payment APIs use the latest technologies to support payment in e-commerce while putting the consumer first. Nike is constantly improving to achieve the most stable and scalable suite of cloud payment services possible.


Features

Support for the most popular payment methods: Pay for an order by gift card, credit card, Apple Pay, PayPal, and more.
Payment allocation across multiple payment methods: Preview how a consumer’s payment is split across multiple payment methods.
Quick and easy to use: Validate and authorize payment in one API call.
PCI-Compliant: Ensure payment information is securely transmitted and stored.


Considerations

API Registration

Complete a one-time registration process in order to begin using the Payment APIs. See the Registration section of the Using NDe APIs guide for more information.

Shipping and Billing Countries

Consider the countries to which your consumers will want to ship Nike products and bill their payments. This determines the payment methods the consumer can use to make a purchase.
