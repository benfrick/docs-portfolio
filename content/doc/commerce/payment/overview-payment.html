Payment Overview

Learn how to use Payment, our solution to allow customers to pay for purchases of Nike products and services.

Nike Payment gives customers fast and easy ways to pay for purchases, with support for popular payment methods across the globe.

Use Cases

Step through the Use Cases below to incorporate Payment capabilities into your experience.

  Get available payment options.
  Get available payment options that the customer has saved.
  Allocate amount owed across payment types.
  Initiate an Apple Pay transaction.
  Initiate a wallet transaction like Paypal Express/Mark.
  Initiate a deferred payment transaction like WeChat.
  Save and validate credit card information. Retrieve masked credit card information.
