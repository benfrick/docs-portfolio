Powering the Co-Creation Generation

Add product customization to your experience using the Customization Experience Platform (CXP).


Integration Overview

CXP is set of APIs and Components that collectively unlock your consumers’ ability to design and buy customized products.

Use Cases

Here are some use cases for integrating CXP capabilities into your experience.

  Show Customizable Products Which products are customizable? How do I start designing?
  Show a Design Experience What customization options are available? What does my design look like? How much will it cost?
  Enable Purchasing Is the product available for purchase? How do I select a size? When would my design be delivered to me?

TIP: See Adding Customization to Your Experience for full integration details.
