Cart and Checkout Overview

Learn how to use Cart and Checkout, our solution to allow customers to purchase Nike products and services.

 

Nike Checkout gives customers fast and easy access to make purchases

Nike builds digital experiences that showcase its premium products and tell amazing stories. Powering those experiences is Nike Checkout, built to handle the massive scale of global e-commerce.
