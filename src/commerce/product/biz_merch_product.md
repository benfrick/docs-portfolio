## <a name="overview"/>Overview

**Nike products drive the e-commerce and brand experience**

The Merchandised Product API provides access to product content and pricing that you can use to tailor your Nike experience to a global audience, making customers feel like they are shopping locally.  The number of ways to slice the data is limitless.

## <a name="features"/>Features

The Merchandised Product API supports the US, Japan, China and European Union geographies, allowing you to reach a wide customer base.

**Here is the kind of data accessible to you by using the Merchandised Product API.**

<i class="g72-plus-large"></i> **Merchandised Products**: Provides detailed information about the product such as style, color, where it can be sold, and if it can be customized.

<i class="g72-plus-large"></i> **Merchandised SKUs**: Gives localized size data, the product a SKU is tied to, where it can be sold and much more.

<i class="g72-plus-large"></i> **Merchandised Prices**: Presents a complete picture of pricing for a product including full price, sale price, employee price, currency and promotion information.

<i class="g72-plus-large"></i> **Merchandised Value-added Services**: Includes add-ons associated with a product such as gift wrap, gift bags and personalization.

<i class="g72-plus-large"></i> **Product Content**: Lists localized content for a product including title, subtitle, description and color to give customers a custom-tailored product experience.

Use the Merchandised Product API to tell your Nike product story.
