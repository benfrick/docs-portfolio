## <a name="overview"></a>Overview

The Nike Payment APIs use the latest technologies to support payment in e-commerce while putting the consumer first. Nike is constantly improving to achieve the most stable and scalable suite of cloud payment services possible.

<br>

![](/images/commerce/payment/snkrs_payment.png)

## <a name="features"></a>Features

<i class="g72-payment-options"></i> **Support for the most popular payment methods**: Pay for an order by gift card, credit card, Apple Pay, PayPal, and more.<br>
<i class="g72-menu"></i> **Payment allocation across multiple payment methods**: Preview how a consumer's payment is split across multiple payment methods.<br>
<i class="g72-play"></i> **Quick and easy to use**: Validate and authorize payment in one API call.<br>
<i class="g72-lock"></i> **PCI-Compliant**: Ensure payment information is securely transmitted and stored.

## <span style="display:inline;">Considerations</span>

**API Registration**

Complete a one-time registration process in order to begin using the Payment APIs. See the Registration section of the [Using NDe APIs](/doc/getting-started/api-registration.html) guide for more information.

**Shipping and Billing Countries**

Consider the countries to which your consumers will want to ship Nike products and bill their payments. This determines the payment methods the consumer can use to make a purchase.
