Need a product grid wall, or customized search results in your experience? Read on to learn about [Rollup Threads](/doc/commerce/product/use-rollup-threads.html).

#### Use Rollup Threads to showcase Nike products using digital product data and authored content.

Great experiences like Nike.com offer many ways to display products to consumers, including the product grid wall. Rollup Threads allows you to do the same, and more:

- **Display related products together**

    Whether to merchandise a grid wall, or for another purpose, get the products you need along with related products in a single API call.
- **Display customized search results**

    Use rules in [Search Admin](https://adminops-int.prod.commerce.nikecloud.com/apollov1/) to curate the results of a consumer's search.
