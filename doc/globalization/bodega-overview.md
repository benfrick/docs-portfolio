h1: Bodega Overview
---

Bodega is an end-to-end string management solution that empowers Nike engineers and producers across the globe to 
easily internationalize their apps.

With Bodega, you can:

- Improve accuracy and relevancy for Nike's international users
- Reduce the time-to-market for new global feature releases and new experiences

>**TIP**: Head over to the [Bodega Sharepoint Site](https://nike.sharepoint.com/sites/ext5/lockerroom/bodega/SitePages/Home.aspx) for comprehensive documentation.

### Bodega Features

- Simple workflow to provide web/mobile apps with localized strings
- Create, translate, and update strings with Cloud-based APIs **at build time**
- Store and manage different variants of strings
- Follow Globalization best practices by separating strings, configurations, URLs, fonts, and screenshots

### Who Uses Bodega?
